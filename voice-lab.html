Signals & Systems concepts jo yahan use hue

  • Equalizer = 5 LTI filters (shelf + peaking biquads) cascade me. Total H(f) = H₁·H₂·H₃·H₄·H₅, jo upar graph me dikhta hai.
  • Pitch change = time scaling x(at). Speed badhao to frequencies bhi scale hoti hain (Fourier scaling property).
  • Robot voice = x(t)·cos(2πf₀t). Time me multiplication = frequency me spectrum shift (modulation property).
  • Echo = y[n] = x[n] + a·y[n−D], ek feedback LTI system with delay.
  • Spectrum = DFT/FFT, jo time domain signal ko frequency domain me dikhata hai.
